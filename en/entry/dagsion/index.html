Dagsion

Feedback / Suggestions

The imperial capital hosting the Heroic Games.

Capital of the Dagdan Empire with upper, middle and lower tiers; a city of over a million people ringed by a vast lake and ruled by the divine pontiff. Facilities per the Korean wiki: Grand Arena (train with allies, watch other clans' bouts), stall street, temple (volunteer for temporary stat or damage-reduction blessings), inn (assign clan members training or gathering, meals) and the perch (switch protagonist; progress is saved per faction). Scouting (retry after raising support), certification exams, expeditions (time passes per move), towns, dungeon exploration and encounters are listed as further actions.
